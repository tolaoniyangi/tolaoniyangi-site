Deconstructed Jenga

A stack of platforms on a city corner that rearranges itself through the day. When it’s quiet they pull apart into small, closed-off places to sit alone. When it’s social they cross into shared spaces for groups. When it’s busy the whole thing opens up.

Core I, Fall 2017. Critic: Tei Carpenter
