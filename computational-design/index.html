05Intelligent City · 2021–22 · Computational design

Mass-timber buildings, generated

Intelligent City designs and manufactures mass-timber housing. Its parametric generator produces buildings the factory can make, and every design is measured for materials, cost and emissions.

Role
Computational designer
Product
Parametric building platform
Status
2021–22, Vancouver
Links
Intelligent City ↗

01The problem

The range of buildings the generator could produce limited what the company could evaluate and optimize. Pulling data out of each design, like material quantities, was manual.

02The work

  • A bigger generator. New building forms, all compatible with the mass-timber manufacturing system.
  • Automatic data extraction from every design, down to material quantities, joined with outside data sources.
  • A client-facing dashboard, designed and built solo, that turns each design into cost and emissions estimates.

03The result

200%more building forms the generator can produce

That range became the base for the company’s design evaluation and optimization, and every design now comes with instant cost and emissions estimates.
