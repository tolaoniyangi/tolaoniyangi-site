05Intelligent City · 2021–22 · Computational design

Mass-timber buildings, generated

Intelligent City designs and manufactures mass-timber housing from a set system of panels. A designer draws a simple line in Rhino, and the generator turns it into a full building made of those panels, floor plans laid out, with its carbon and cost estimated automatically.

Role
Computational designer
Product
Parametric building platform
Timeline
2021–22
Links
Intelligent City ↗

01Context

Intelligent City builds with a set system of mass-timber panels made in its own factory. Every design has to be buildable from those panels, which makes designing by hand slow, and makes it hard to compare options on carbon and cost early on.

02Approach

  • A line in, a building out. A designer draws a simple line in Rhino, a few segments long. The generator turns it into a full building made from the set panel system, with every floor plan already laid out. This work increased the range of generatable building forms by 200%.
  • Every design, measured. Quantities of timber, concrete and steel are pulled out of each generated design automatically and joined with outside data.
  • A carbon and cost dashboard, designed and built solo, that turns those quantities into embodied carbon and cost estimates for clients.

03Outcome

200%increase in the range of generatable building forms

Designers could go from a sketch to a buildable design with carbon and cost numbers in one step, and compare options while they still mattered. That range became the base for the company’s design evaluation and optimization at the time.
