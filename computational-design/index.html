05Intelligent City · Vancouver · Computational design

Mass-timber buildings, generated

Intelligent City designs and manufactures mass-timber housing. I grew the software that generates its buildings and measured what each one would cost to make.

Role
Computational designer
Company
Intelligent City ↗
When
2021–2022
Tools
Grasshopper, Rhino, Python, C#

01More buildings

I expanded the parametric building generator so it could produce 200% more building forms, all compatible with the company’s mass-timber manufacturing system. That range became the base for the company’s design evaluation and optimization.

02Every design, measured

I automated pulling building data out of every design, down to material quantities, and joined it with outside data sources. Then I designed and built, on my own, a client-facing dashboard that turned it into instant cost and emissions estimates.
