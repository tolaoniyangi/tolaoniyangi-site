01The question

Before a site can be graded, someone has to estimate how much soil comes out and how much goes back in. It’s one of the first numbers on any construction project, and it usually takes survey data and CAD software.

02What it does

Pick or draw a site in Earth. Cut and fill finds the elevation where cut equals fill, or calculates the volumes for any target elevation you set, and maps where the earth has to move.

03My part

I designed and implemented the back end: the calculations behind the volumes and the cut and fill map the interface draws.

It’s one of four terrain analysis tools now live in Google Earth.
