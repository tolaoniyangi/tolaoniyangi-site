06BallotReady · Fall 2020 · Data visualization

Election data for 5M voters

BallotReady is a nonpartisan voter guide used by more than 5 million people in 2020. On its data team, I checked where and when people could vote, and charted what was on the ballot.

Role
Elections intern, data team
When
Fall 2020
Company
BallotReady ↗
Used by

01Getting it right

I collected and verified hundreds of early voting, absentee and polling place locations and hours from local election offices across the US for the 2020 general election.

02Making it readable

  • Top issues by state, 2018 vs 2020, for What are candidates talking about?
  • Ballot trends by state across education, criminal justice, environment, healthcare and more, for What’s on the ballot?
  • Voting options available before election day, for Today is the day
