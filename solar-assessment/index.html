Solar layouts and potential

Estimate a site’s solar potential in minutes: a panel layout for any building or parking lot, with expected output and cost.

Role

Status

01The problem

Planners and developers want to know whether solar makes sense on a site before they hire anyone. A useful estimate needs a layout that could actually be built.

02The work

  • Solar API integration. The first in Google Earth, for residential and small commercial roofs up to 500 m².
  • Placement logic that keeps panels clear of HVAC units and fire-code setbacks.
  • Panel types by building size, residential or commercial, so output estimates stay realistic.
  • Beyond rooftops. Design logic for solar canopies, and production code for carports.

03The result

Solar studies are live in Google Earth for rooftops and parking lots, with three carport options and expected output, savings and payback for each design.
