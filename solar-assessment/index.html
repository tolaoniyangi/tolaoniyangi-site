Solar layouts for real roofs

Pick a building or a parking lot and get a solar layout that respects the real site, with cost and output, in minutes.

01The question

Planners and developers want to know if solar makes sense on a site before they hire anyone. Answering that well means panels have to fit the actual roof, not an idealized box.

02What I did

  • Led the first Solar API integration in Google Earth, for residential and small commercial roofs up to 500 m².
  • Wrote placement logic that accounts for roof segments, obstructions, HVAC, slope, access aisles and local codes.
  • Switched panel types between residential and commercial by building size, so yield estimates stay realistic.
  • Went past rooftops. I designed the logic for solar canopies and worked on the production code for carports.

03Parking lots

Carports give professionals three ways to turn a parking lot into an energy source, each with its own financials.
